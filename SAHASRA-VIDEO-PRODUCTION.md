## 2. Voiceover script (optional VO — or use as on-screen text only)
Deliver flat and calm; let the visuals carry energy. Pauses matter more than pace.

> **[Ch1]** Eleven hundred police stations. And no way to see if they're connected. *(beat)* Three thefts, three districts, one gang — and the data never talks to itself. *(the lines connect)* So we built the connection.
> **[Ch2]** KSP already has a chatbot. We didn't build another one. We built something a jury understands in three seconds — not something they have to type a good question into.
> **[Ch3]** One platform. *(montage)* Every connection here is real — confirmed links solid, AI-suggested links dashed — and you can trace the path between any two. Drag the forecast forward and watch tomorrow's risk before it happens. A camera match reaches a supervisor in seconds, not next week's report. Every score, every flag, explained in plain language — never a black box. We built the accountability layer most teams skip. One flagged case moves through four roles as one connected chain. The same intelligence rides in an officer's pocket. And we built for the humans doing the job — fatigue, a silent duress signal, a wellness check after a hard call.
> **[Ch4]** This isn't a mockup. Every screen is real, running, on real data. And it's built to deploy on Zoho Catalyst — not a demo that dies after the pitch.
> **[Ch5]** *(quiet)* A pattern caught two days earlier. An officer who comes home safe. That's what this is actually for. *(beat)* SAHASRA.
